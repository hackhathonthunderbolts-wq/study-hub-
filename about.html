About

What Study Hub is — and what it is not

Study Hub is a student-built fan companion: a tidy place to find free learning content, plus a small newsletter of study tips.

Plainly: this is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or operated by Vedantu. It does not sell courses, coaching or anything else. Everything here is free.

What you can do here

  • Browse free lessons by class or exam and by subject, using filters — no account needed.
  • Join the study-tips newsletter for short, practical revision notes.
  • Request a topic you would like explained, and see what others recently asked for.

Where the lessons come from

Video players on this site load from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you press play. The lesson titles on this site are our own summaries of common syllabus topics — they are not official titles, and the video IDs are placeholders until an editor fills them in.

Vedantu publishes a large amount of free educational content on YouTube. To watch it, go straight to the source:

Official website — vedantu.com Official YouTube channel

What we collect (and what we never ask for)

  • Newsletter: first name (optional), email, language preference, the classes you tick, and your consent. Used only to send the newsletter.
  • Topic requests: name and email are both optional; the request text and two dropdowns. Used only as feedback.
  • Never: phone numbers, school names, addresses, marks, photos or payment details. Please do not put personal details in free-text fields.
  • Unsubscribe: every newsletter email carries an unsubscribe link, and you can ask for your details to be deleted at any time.

Facts we have not verified

To keep this project honest, anything we could not check is marked:

[VERIFY: subscriber count] [VERIFY: number of free videos] [VERIFY: official contact address]

If you are editing this site, replace each mark with a checked source — or delete the sentence. We deliberately do not publish statistics, rankings, results, pricing or teacher biographies here.

Credits and thanks

Designed and built as a fan project by students. All artwork, icons and the Study Hub wordmark are original and made for this site; no logos or brand assets of Vedantu are used. Kind regards to the teachers whose free videos make self-study possible for millions of students.
